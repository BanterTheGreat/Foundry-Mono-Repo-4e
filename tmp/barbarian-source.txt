A Sharp Sword And A Straight Path: The Barbarian 2.0

Barbarian 2.0 – Updated Primal Content
Class Traits
Role: Striker. You use powerful two-handed weapons to deal serious damage to your enemies. Your physical power and daunting presence can cause foes to cower before you, and you can temporarily increase your abilities by harnessing great bursts of terrifying rage. Depending on your choice of class features and powers, you lean toward either defender or leader as a secondary role.
Power Source: Primal. You are a primal champion, a warrior devoted to the natural world and an embodiment of your tribe's fierce traditions.
Key Abilities: Strength, Constitution, Dexterity, Charisma

Armor Proficiencies: Cloth, leather, hide.
Weapon Proficiencies: Simple melee, simple ranged, military melee.
Bonus to Defense: +2 class bonus to Fortitude.

Hit Points at 1st Level: 15+ Constitution Score.
Hit Points per Level Gained: 6
Healing Surges per Day: 8+ Constitution Modifier.

Trained Skills: From the class skills list below, choose 4 trained skills at 1st level.
Class Skills: Acrobatics (Dex), Athletics (Str), Endurance (Con), Heal (Wis), Intimidate (Cha), Nature (Wis), Perception (Wis), Thievery (Dex).

Build Options: Rageblood Barbarian, Thaneborn Barbarian, Thunderborn Barbarian, Whirling Barbarian.
Class features: Barbarian Agility, Feral Might, Rage Strike, Rampage.
Class Features
BARBARIAN AGILITY
While wearing light or no armor, you gain a +1 bonus to your Reflex defense and may use your Strength modifier in place of Intelligence or Dexterity when calculating your AC score.
FERAL MIGHT
Barbarians connect with the natural world in a variety of ways. Some barbarians grow so hardened to physical punishment that they find it easier to simply absorb, rather than avoid, attacks. Others are living examples of the power of one's will to shape one's fate. Choose one of the following options. The choice you make gives you the benefit described below and also provides bonuses to certain barbarian powers, as detailed in those powers.

Rageblood Vigor

RAGE STRIKE
Barbarian daily attack powers have the Rage keyword. They allow you to unleash powerful bursts of emotion, willpower, and primal energy. Each rage power starts with a mighty attack, and then you enter a rage, which grants an ongoing benefit.

You additionally gain the rage strike power, which lets you channel your excess rage into a devastating attack while you're raging. Using rage strike is an alternative to using a second rage power in a climactic battle; it gives you the damage output of a daily power without forcing you to enter a different rage.

 Rage Strike										Barbarian Attack 
You channel your primal rage into a devastating attack.
At-Will (Special)   ✦     					Primal, Weapon
Standard Action     					Melee weapon
Requirement: You must be raging and have at least one unused rage power.
Target: One creature
Attack: Strength vs. AC. To make this attack, you expend an unused Daily Barbarian rage power without using it.
Hit: You deal damage based on the level of the rage power you expended:
1st level: 	3[W] + Strength modifier
5th level: 	5[W] + Strength modifier
9th level:	7[W] + Strength modifier
15th level: 	9[W] + Strength modifier
19th level: 	11[W] + Strength modifier
20th level:	12[W] + Strength modifier
25th level: 	13[W] + Strength modifier
29th level: 	15[W] + Strength modifier
Miss: Half damage.
Special: If the target of this power is in the Elite or Solo group, the total damage you deal to that target is doubled.

RAMPAGE

Barbarian 2.0 – Updated Elemental Content
Berserker Alternative: The Heartland Hero Barbarian
Heroes of the Feywild ambitiously attempted to implement a dual-role, mode-switching defender subclass for the Barbarian, named the Berserker. This design was unfortunately poorly conceived on too many levels for any simple fix or buff cycle through its powers or features to make it function as intended. I will instead attempt to accomplish the same goal here, while exploring similar thematic concepts, by turning the Berserker's design inside-out.
The Heartland Hero Barbarian creates a dual-role, mode-switching Barbarian through the use of two alternative class features and associated power support. The first of these class features is a Feral Might replacement, which may be taken separately and influences the Barbarian's power source. The second replaces Rampage and changes the Barbarian's role to include defender, but requires selecting powers compatible with these defender features, primarily by relying on the Elemental source keyword.
Heartland Might
This class feature may be taken by any Barbarian as an alternative to Feral Might. A Barbarian that takes this feature treats Barbarian as a class of both the Primal and Elemental power sources. Barbarians with the Heartland Might option are indelibly marked by the elemental nature of their native territory, and can draw on its essence for strength to protect their new-found allies. Choose one of the following home territories and gain the associated power and benefits.

Might of the Arid Desert
The Arid Desert Heartland is associated with elemental fire, dual-wielding, and ambush tactics. Powers with Arid Desert riders emphasize bursts of mobility, engage multiple enemies, and some create illusions. Your secondary stat is Dexterity, and you may wish to take training in Stealth.
You gain the Desert's Wrath power, and you can wield a one-handed weapon in your off hand and treat it as an off-hand weapon. While you are wearing cloth armor or no armor and wielding a weapon in each hand, you gain a +3 bonus to AC and a +1 bonus to Reflex. You have resistance to fire damage equal to your Dexterity modifier plus half your level, and a +2 bonus to fire damage rolls. Increase the bonus to fire damage rolls to +3 at 11th level and +4 at 21st level.

Your secondary stat is Dexterity, and you may wish to obtain a method of quickly switching between your melee and ranged weapons.
You gain the Steppe's Wrath power. You have resistance to lightning damage equal to your Dexterity modifier plus half your level, and a +2 bonus to lightning damage rolls. Increase the bonus to lightning damage rolls to +3 at 11th level and +4 at 21st level.

This class feature may be taken by any Barbarian as an alternative to Rampage. A Barbarian that takes this feature treats Barbarian as a class of both the Striker and Defender roles. Barbarians with the Hero's Heart option rage against the will of the world when they use Elemental power, visibly warping the space around them into a likeness of the Barbarian's natural domain. You gain the following powers and benefits.
Pulse of Heroism
The first time each round that each enemy you have marked makes an attack that does not include you as a target, that enemy takes damage equal to your Strength modifier plus half your level. This damage is of the same type you resist due to your Heartland Might, if applicable. You have the Hero's Aura power.
