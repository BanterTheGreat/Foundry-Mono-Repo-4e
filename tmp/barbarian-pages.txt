BARBARIAN
“My strength is the fury of the wild.”
What are they?
Barbarians are savage warriors who deal out powerful blows 
from their mighty weapons. They charge from foe to foe and 
seldom feel the pain of an enemy’s strike. For barbarians’ foes, 
the moments of greatest terror come when barbarians call 
upon primal forces to lend power to their raging spirits. 
These rages, although temporary, give a barbarian incredible 
power–a combination of skill, willpower, and a legacy of 
ancient tribal rituals.
As a barbarian, you have a link to powerful nature spirits and 
other primal forces bound to the warriors of your tribe by the 
songs and totems of your legacy. These spirits lend energy to 
your rages, transforming you into a devastating force on the 
battlefield. As you become more experienced, these rages 
transcend mortal limitations, manifesting directly as waves of 
elemental power or gifting you with supernatural 
recuperative powers.
Gameplay Role
Barbarian is a Strength -based Primal Striker. Charge 
everywhere, hit hard, yell. One of the tougher Strikers, but no 
slouch on damage.

Rage Strike
Barbarian daily attack powers with the Rage keyword begin with an attack 
and let you enter a rage, granting an ongoing benefit until the rage ends. A 
rage lasts until the end of the encounter, until you enter another rage, or until 
you fall unconscious.

for a single attack while keeping your current rage.

Barbarian Paragon Paths
Also see Zeitgeist Paragon Paths on page 187 for additional paths available to any class that meets their prerequisites.
○ Ancestral Weapon 
Draw the enemy's attention with the strength of the ancestors behind 
you, marking foes and making your exchanges with them increasingly 
punishing. Charisma deepens your healing reserves, and a successful 
action point attack lets you spend a healing surge to stay in the fight. 
As your bond grows, Rampage can carry an at-will technique into the 
opening, while your greatest rage adds force to every blow against an 
enemy you have marked.
○ Bear Warrior 
Take on a bear's shape when you rage, gaining tougher protection and 
the freedom to catch your breath with second wind without slowing 
your assault. Action points offer further healing, while mauling blows 
knock enemies down and threaten another strike when they stand 
beside you. Your growing strength sends foes flying on a raging critical 
hit, and your fiercest rage carries you between opponents before 
settling into steady regeneration and resistance to being moved.
○ Nocturnal 
Hunt through the boundary between the world and its shadows, passing 
through obstacles as you charge a visible enemy. An action point spent 
on a successful at-will attack can send you straight into another charge, 
while a brutal follow-up blow grants temporary vitality. Shadow 
packmates flank enemies alongside your companions, and your later 
gifts let you spend a healing surge to move through solid barriers or 
leave lingering necrotic and poisonous wounds with every melee hit.
○ Winter Fury 
Freeze enemies in place and make their helpless footing count, striking 
immobilized foes with greater accuracy and force. Your action point 
attacks can lock down a cluster of enemies, while a mantle of glacial 
armor absorbs punishment until repeated blows or fire break it apart. 
With experience, you resist the cold and can turn ordinary damage into 
frost, and your greatest rage lets each at-will hit hold its victim in place 
for another assault.
○ Stonefire Rager 
Endure with the patience of stone, then erupt in a rush of heat and 
violence. You can turn some critical hits against you into ordinary 
wounds, and an action point attack adds Constitution -driven fire 
damage while knocking its victims down. Remaining outside a rage lets 
you shelter behind broad damage resistance, while being bloodied 
brings fire resistance and harder blows. When you finally unleash your 
volcanic rage, nearby enemies burn each turn and those that strike you 
leave themselves open to your answer.
○ Calm Fury 
Focus your anger into deliberate Rage Strikes, using them even before 
you enter a rage and eventually putting greater force behind each blow. 
Spending an action point restores health, and a precise strike against 
Reflex lets you draw on a healing surge. A critical hit can turn Rampage 
into another use of that healing attack, while your greatest rage steadies 
the accuracy of at-will attacks and Rage Strikes and rewards their hits 
with temporary vitality.
