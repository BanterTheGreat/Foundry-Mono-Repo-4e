# Murk — Equipment Options, Levels 10–12

Murk is a creature-handler with a dangerous personal perimeter: his companions apply pressure, while enemies who bypass them meet sticky bombs, retaliation, and an unpleasant surprise.

Fit divisions apply independently: **3 Close / 1 Adjacent / 1 Wild** for armor and staffs; **7 Close / 2 Adjacent / 1 Wild** for other equipment. These are alternatives, rather than a single loadout. Published mechanics were checked against the repository's offline D&D 4e compendium.

## Armor — 5 options

All selections are available as **leather**, compatible with standard shaman proficiency.

| Item | Level | Fit | Effect / why choose it |
|---|---:|---|---|
| **Deathburst Armor +3** | 12 | Close | **Daily, minor:** until your next turn ends, creatures hitting you with melee attacks take **2d8 + Constitution modifier necrotic damage**. The clearest “coming near me hurts” option. Also automatically saves against ongoing necrotic damage. |
| **Deathcut Armor +2** | 10 | Close | **Resist 5 necrotic and poison. Daily, immediate reaction:** after an enemy hits you in melee, deal **1d10 + Charisma modifier necrotic damage** to it. A concealed retaliation, though its damage depends partly on Charisma. |
| **Warding Spirit Armor +3** | 12 | Close | **+4 power bonus to AC against opportunity attacks** while your spirit companion is present. Helps Murk slip away and leave an approaching enemy dealing with his creatures again. |
| **Armor of Resistance +3** | 12 | Adjacent | **Resist 10** against one eligible damage type fixed when created: acid, cold, fire, force, lightning, necrotic, poison, psychic, or thunder. Choose around the campaign's recurring threats. |
| **Ever-Fading Armor +2** | 10 | Wild | **At-will, standard:** extend existing invisibility until your next turn ends. Enables elusive scouting and ambush play, but **does not grant initial invisibility** and costs actions to maintain. |

## Staff implements — 5 options

| Item | Level | Fit | Effect / why choose it |
|---|---:|---|---|
| **Aversion Staff +3** | 12 | Close | **+2 item bonus to all defenses** against enemies currently subject to effects Murk caused. His disruptive powers can make attacking the handler harder. Requires an ongoing applicable effect on that enemy. |
| **Staff of the Traveler +2** | 10 | Close | Replace shifts with **teleports of the same distance**; critical hits teleport the target **4 squares**. Excellent for escaping a crowded perimeter. Its separate encounter power requires psionic teleportation. |
| **Staff of Knives +3** | 12 | Close | **At-will, minor:** transform the staff into a dagger or back. Deals **+3d12 critical damage when used as a melee weapon**. A literal surprise blade, but its value depends on Murk having a workable weapon attack. It supplies no new attack power. |
| **Staff of the Viper +3** | 12 | Adjacent | **Daily, no action after hitting with an attack using the staff:** inflict **ongoing 10 poison damage, save ends**. Adds a nasty personal sting, although it rewards Murk attacking rather than commanding creatures. |
| **Staff of Tongues +3** | 12 | Wild | **+1 item bonus to Diplomacy** and literacy and speech in **Supernal**. Lets the creature-handler branch into supernatural negotiation. |

If “surprise bliky” specifically means a **concealed firearm**, Staff of Knives captures the surprise-weapon idea only. It is mechanically a transforming dagger.

## Priorities and qualifications

Start with **Deathburst Armor**, **Aversion Staff**, **Flask of the Veiled Horde**, and **Antipathy Gloves**, backed by **Tanglefoot Bags**. This gives Murk a creature to deploy, a harder-to-reach personal space, and punishment for enemies who get through.

A **spirit companion is not automatically a summoned creature**. Keep Summoner's Staff off the main list until his actual powers confirm that qualification. Ranged and area alchemical attacks normally provoke opportunity attacks: throw them while enemies approach, before they reach him.

Standard permanent-item prices for these selections are **5,000 gp at level 10**, **9,000 gp at level 11**, and **13,000 gp at level 12**. Consumable prices are listed separately above.
