The Salt Ledger. The party catches a smuggling crew checking its last shipment before departure. Their foreman stands watch over the shipping ledger while a hired ogre drops a crate and reaches for his mallet. Raid the warehouse, overcome the guards, and seize the ledger. The lanes between cargo stacks give the party several ways to reach the shooter.

Objective: Ledger at (13, 3).
Escape: East loading doors onto the quay. PCs may instead deploy along the southern quay if approaching by boat.

Enemy objectives

The smugglers want to drive the intruders away and keep their ledger. If the enforcer falls and either the ogre or shooter is defeated, surviving smugglers surrender if cornered or flee onto the quay; the ogre follows their lead.

Tactics

The enforcer meets the first intruder through the western doors while the toughs charge and spread around that target. A successful charge grants combat advantage, letting the enforcer's Duelist’s Poise pin someone in place. He uses Advantageous Jab when a marked enemy tries to attack his crew.

The ogre circles the central stack toward anyone pushing past the enforcer. He uses Juggernaut Push along a clear aisle to knock a target away from support, then keeps them busy with his mallet. He likes breaking crates but avoids the water.

The shooter opens with Bullseye Bolt against an exposed intruder and relocates around the northern stacks when threatened. Toughs try to flank rather than hold a formation; once several fall, the remaining guards close around the ledger.

Special rules — included

Cargo: Waist-high crates provide normal cover when they intervene; tall stacks block sight and movement. Both are fixed obstacles for this simple encounter. Ledger: An adjacent creature can pick it up as a minor action. There is no countdown. Water: The harbour is 5 feet below the quay; use normal falling, forced-movement saving throws, and swimming rules if someone goes over. A dock ladder at (16, 12) lets a swimmer climb back onto the quay using movement; adjudicate movement manually. No environmental damage or added XP is included.

Monster stats

Adaptations: the enforcer and toughs are level-adjusted human duelist/thug profiles; the shooter is an adjusted human archer. HP, defenses, attacks, and damage use the saved monster-math table. The ogre retains its combat values. Names and italic flavor are adapted. All are standard rank unless marked minion; no action points or saving-throw bonuses.

A · Warehouse enforcer

Level 11 soldier · 600 XP · Medium natural humanoid (human). HP 79; bloodied 39. AC 27; Fort 23, Ref 23, Will 23. Speed 6; initiative +12; Perception +8; normal senses. Str 16 (+8), Con 13 (+6), Dex 20 (+10), Int 10 (+5), Wis 17 (+8), Cha 9 (+4). Athletics +13. Unaligned; Common. Leather armor, longsword.

Duelist’s Poise — trait. He catches your sleeve on his blade and holds you where he wants you. Whenever he hits an enemy granting combat advantage to him, that enemy is immobilized until the end of its next turn.

Longsword — standard, at-will, weapon; melee basic. The foreman drives his blade between the cargo stacks. Melee 1, one creature; +16 vs. AC; 3d6 + 9 damage. Effect: target is marked until the end of the enforcer’s next turn.

Advantageous Jab — immediate interrupt, at-will, weapon. As you turn away, his sword flashes at your ribs. Trigger: an enemy marked by him makes an attack that does not include him. Melee 1, triggering enemy; +16 vs. AC; 2d6 + 6 damage. Effect: target takes −2 to attack rolls until the end of this turn. Limited by normal immediate-action rules.

B · Ogre cargo-handler

Level 10 brute · 500 XP · Large natural humanoid (giant). HP 131; bloodied 65. AC 22; Fort 24, Ref 20, Will 20. Speed 8; initiative +7; Perception +7; normal senses. Str 24 (+12), Con 21 (+10), Dex 15 (+7), Int 4 (+2), Wis 15 (+7), Cha 6 (+3). Chaotic evil; Giant. Packing mallet (greatclub), four rocks.

Packing Mallet — standard, at-will, weapon; melee basic. A mallet the size of a barrel head sweeps toward you. Melee 2, one creature; +15 vs. AC; 4d8 + 5 damage.

Rock — standard, at-will; ranged basic. The ogre snatches a ballast stone and hurls it down the aisle. Ranged 5, one creature; +15 vs. AC; 3d6 + 7 damage.

Juggernaut Push — standard, recharge 5–6. He lowers a shoulder and ploughs you backwards across the boards. Melee 1, one creature; +13 vs. Fortitude. Hit: push target 1 square and knock it prone; ogre shifts 1 square into its vacated space. He can push it up to 8 additional squares, shifting equally and remaining adjacent; target takes 1d8 damage per additional square pushed. Normal legal movement and footprint restrictions apply.

C · Crossbow shooter

Level 11 artillery · 600 XP · Medium natural humanoid (human). HP 54; bloodied 27. AC 23; Fort 23, Ref 23, Will 23. Speed 6; initiative +8; Perception +6; normal senses. Str 14 (+7), Con 14 (+7), Dex 16 (+8), Int 12 (+6), Wis 12 (+6), Cha 13 (+6). Unaligned; Common. Club, crossbow, 20 bolts.

Club — standard, at-will, weapon; melee basic. He swings a belaying pin and glances toward the nearest exit. Melee 1, one creature; +16 vs. AC; 1d6 + 6 damage.

Crossbow — standard, at-will, weapon; ranged basic. A bolt hisses through the gap between two crates. Ranged 15/30, one creature; +16 vs. AC; 3d6 + 9 damage.

Bullseye Bolt — standard, recharge 5–6, weapon. He braces the crossbow on a crate and aims for your head. Ranged 15/30, one creature; +16 vs. AC; 3d6 + 13 damage, and dazed (save ends).

D · Dock tough

Level 11 minion skirmisher · 150 XP each · Medium natural humanoid (human). HP 1; a missed attack never damages a minion. AC 25; Fort 23, Ref 23, Will 23. Speed 6; initiative +7; Perception +6; normal senses. Str 14 (+7), Con 13 (+6), Dex 11 (+5), Int 10 (+5), Wis 12 (+6), Cha 13 (+6). Unaligned; Common. Club.

Rush into Battle — trait. The tough barrels into you, leaving you off balance amid the cargo. Whenever the tough hits with a charge attack, the target grants combat advantage until the end of that tough’s next turn.

Club — standard, at-will, weapon; melee basic. A scarred dockhand brings a weighted club down hard. Melee 1, one creature; +16 vs. AC; 10 damage.

Conversion damage notes: Enforcer sword and shooter bolt average 19.5; jab averages 13 as a secondary reactive attack. Bullseye averages 23.5, approximately the +25% limited-power budget; club averages 9.5 as its weak fallback. Toughs deal the level-11 minion profile of 10. The enforcer’s extra reactive damage and the shooter’s recurring daze retain their original ability structure; these are conversion judgments.
