Throwaway design study, iteration 14. Actions are now icon-only buttons next to the character name instead of a separate column — Use AP shows an AP-count badge, Save/Heal/Rest still open dropdowns. Iteration 13 is kept as horizontal-hud-prototype.backup-iter13.html.

Save → Saving Throw, Death Save (disabled unless dying). Heal → Second Wind, Spend Surge (disabled at 0 surges). Rest → Short Rest, Extended Rest. The segmented bar under the Temp HP/Surges chips tracks the surge fraction (10/12 → most of the bar lit).
