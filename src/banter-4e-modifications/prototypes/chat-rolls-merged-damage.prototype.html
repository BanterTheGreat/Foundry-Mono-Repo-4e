When does the bonus surface?

An outcome-changing bonus glows green; an outcome-changing penalty glows red. Otherwise, temporary attack modifiers stay inside the details.

Damage remembers its source

The total sits beside the power that caused it. Temporary damage contributions stay visible: here, Flame infusion adds 2d8 + 2 fire damage. Its rolled contribution is in the tooltip and breakdown. Permanent equipment bonuses stay inside. The damage total appears once, inside the action. After applying, the total stays visible and the same control offers Undo Damage.

Open any row

The whole reminder is the collapser. Hover, focus, or tap a total to see the natural d20. Open the row for every modifier, equipment, and damage types. The merged damage control stays visible while collapsed. Click its number to apply; click again to undo. Attack reminders have no action buttons.
