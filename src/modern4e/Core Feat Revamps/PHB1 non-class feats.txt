Benefit: You may choose to make any untyped damage you would deal Fire damage. You ignore resistance and immunity to damage whenever you deal Fire damage. The first time each turn when you score a critical hit with a power that has the Fire keyword, you must pull every creature that can see you up to 2 squares. This is a Charm effect. Any creature adjacent to you after the pull that was not a target of the triggering power takes damage as if it were hit (but not critically hit) by the triggering power. Increase the distance of the pull to up to 3 squares at 21st level.
Published in Player's Handbook, page(s) 207.

Light Blade Precision
Paragon Tier
Prerequisite: 11th level
Benefit: You gain a +2 bonus to damage rolls with daggers and light blades against creatures of size Large or larger. At 21st level this bonus increases to +3.
Published in Player's Handbook, page(s) 204.

Lightning Arc
Paragon Tier
Prerequisite: 11th level, CON 15 or DEX 15
Benefit: You may choose to make any untyped damage you would deal Lightning damage. Once per turn when you score a critical hit with a power that has the Lightning keyword, you may choose another creature within 10 squares of the original target that was not a target of the triggering power. That creature takes damage as if it were hit (but not critically hit) by the triggering power.
Published in Player's Handbook, page(s) 204.

Occult Reach
Paragon Tier
Prerequisite: 11th level, DEX 15 or INT 15
Benefit: When using a close implement attack power, you can choose a square within 2 squares of yours as the origin square. You are still excluded from the area of the attack. At 21st level, you can choose a square within 3 squares of your square.
Formerly Arcane Reach. Published in Player's Handbook, page(s) 202.

Polearm Gamble
Paragon Tier
Prerequisite: 11th level, STR 15 or WIS 15
Benefit: When a nonadjacent enemy enters a square adjacent to you, you can make an opportunity attack with a polearm against that enemy, but you grant combat advantage to that enemy until the end of the enemy's turn.
Published in Player's Handbook, page(s) 204.

Psychic Lock
Paragon Tier
Prerequisite: 11th level, WIS 15 or CHA 15
Benefit: Any target you hit with a power that has the psychic keyword takes a –2 penalty to its next attack roll.
Published in Player's Handbook, page(s) 205.

Secret Stride
Paragon Tier
Prerequisite: 11th level, trained in Stealth
Benefit: Whenever you take the Walk action, you may shift 1 square as a free action prior to walking. You never take the –5 penalty to Stealth checks for moving more than 2 squares as part of the same action.
Published in Player's Handbook, page(s) 205.

Wintertouched
Paragon Tier
Prerequisite: 11th level, CON 15 or INT 15
Benefit: You may choose to make any untyped damage you would deal Cold damage. You gain cold resistance equal to 5 + half your level, or your highest resistance to cold increases by 5. You have combat advantage against creatures vulnerable to Cold. You gain the Cold keyword and cannot take any options requiring a pact with the Summer Fey.
Published in Player's Handbook, page(s) 201.

Prerequisite: 21st level
Benefit: While you are wielding two melee weapons with which you are proficient, you may choose for your Opportunity Attack to have the modified Effect: You make a melee basic attack against the target using each of your main hand weapon and your off-hand weapon.

Unfettered Stride
Epic Tier
Prerequisite: 21st level, trained in Athletics
Benefit: You can use the Escape action to end any condition that slows, immobilizes, or restrains you. When you succeed in an Escape check, you can shift your speed. You can also take the Escape action at the start of your turn to end a condition that removes you from play. You return to play in your previous space, or the nearest unoccupied space.
See Squeeze Through Shadows for prior effect. Published in Player's Handbook, page(s) 207.
