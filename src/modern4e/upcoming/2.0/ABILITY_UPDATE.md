# Ability Advancement

The current 2.0 proposal uses Modern 4e ability points and follows the timing of vanilla 4e modifier increases for a character who starts with even scores and keeps raising the same pair.

| Level | Ability increase |
| --- | --- |
| 8, 14, 28 | Choose two different abilities and increase each by 1. |
| 21 | Increase all six abilities by 1. |

The two picks can include your main ability. You can choose the same pair at each milestone or change your picks.

## Example: Idrani

Idrani is a Strength-based Fighter. If she chooses Strength and Constitution at each opportunity, her gains from advancement are:

| Level | Strength | Constitution | Each other ability | Total points gained |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 8 | +1 | +1 | +0 | +2 |
| 14 | +2 | +2 | +0 | +4 |
| 21 | +3 | +3 | +1 | +10 |
| 28 | +4 | +4 | +1 | +12 |

With even starting scores and repeated Strength and Constitution picks, vanilla 4e gives Idrani the same modifier gains at the same levels. Odd starting scores or different picks can change when vanilla modifiers rise. The proposed Modern 4e track uses the same milestones for every character.

## Deferred: Level 1 Ability Boosts

Keep the existing level 1 ability assignment for 2.0. The boost sequence and theme ability pairs below are retained for reference.

Try a Pathfinder 2e-style boost sequence for level 1. The chosen theme fills the slot Pathfinder gives to ancestry.

Start all six abilities at +0. Each boost adds 1 point. You can boost the same ability in different steps.

| Step | Ability boosts |
| --- | --- |
| Theme | Boost one of the theme's two listed abilities, then boost any different ability. |
| Background | Boost one of the background's two listed abilities, then boost any different ability. |
| Class | Boost your main ability. |
| Free boosts | Boost four different abilities of your choice. |

This gives nine boosts. Boosting one ability in all four steps brings it to +4. The theme's free boost can go into your class's main ability whenever you used the listed boost on a different ability.
