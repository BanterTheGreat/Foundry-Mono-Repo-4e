## Order of events

1. **Spring 8 - Royal briefing and opening attacks.** Kell's thugs ambush the PCs' carriage en route to King Aodhan. The king orders them to expose the Obscurati operation beneath Cauldron Hill before the peace summit. The B-Team collects Brakken of Heffanita, the Beran mediator. Asrabey Varal requests help stopping the rogue fey lord Ekossigan. Dwarven radicals launch a bombing and sniper attack around Pardwight University. (pp. 348-359)

2. **Spring 9 - The investigation opens up.** The repaired bronze golem reveals Alexander Grappa's surviving consciousness; he can help with the Cauldron Hill mission, though a geas restricts his disclosures. A warning letter from Vlendam Heid and an invitation from Captain Dale arrive. The PCs receive a fifty-officer task force to dismantle Kell's guild. Early Danoran dignitaries begin arriving. (pp. 343, 360-363, 384)

3. **Spring 10 - Leads and a dangerous shipment.** Asrabey asks to meet Gale. That evening the B-Team escorts Brakken to Sunset Bench, one of Kell's fronts. Late that night the eschatologists bring a shipload of monsters into Flint; the PCs can intercept it. (pp. 367-370, 372-374, 388-390)

4. **Spring 11 - Threats become visible.** Before dawn, any successfully landed monsters rampage, then hide in lairs. In the morning Brakken and the B-Team witness the paranoia spreading through the Cloudwood under Ekossigan's curse. The evening opera offers a chance to arrest Kell's lawyer, Quentin Augst. (pp. 363-365, 375-376; schedule p. 463)

5. **Spring 12 - Two major deadlines.** In the morning Grundun Zubov attacks the subrail opening and takes hostages; his ultimate plan is to send an explosive-laden train beneath the summit dignitaries' hotel. At sunset the Cloudwood mob murders its mayor unless stopped. Just before midnight Ekossigan intends to sacrifice thirty-one children at Gallo's School for Boys to open a route into the Bleak Gate. Stopping him can reveal that Kasvarina is beneath Cauldron Hill and secure Asrabey's help with the raid. (pp. 378-394)

6. **Spring 13 - Kell's last opening and the summit banquet.** At about 3 a.m., Kell leaves his hideout, giving the PCs another chance to catch him. If he escapes, he can murder the B-Team and flee to the Obscurati facility. Han and Lya Jierre arrive for the summit; the evening banquet lets the PCs question Lya socially. Asrabey interrupts to request permission to enter the Bleak Gate. Afterward Brakken reveals that a Danoran is hurrying to a secret portal to warn the defenders: following him offers an immediate route in. If necessary, Aodhan can send the party across just after midnight, at the start of Spring 14. (pp. 370-371, 395-402)

7. **The Bleak Gate raid - The hidden factory is exposed.** The PCs infiltrate the shadow version of Cauldron Hill, with allies as available. Asrabey seeks Kasvarina; Grappa wants to reach the colossus. They reach Borne's construction chamber and confront the surviving defenders, potentially including Kell and Leone Quital. Borne awakens and breaks free; the precise trigger varies. The collapse breaks the wards and shifts the facility and colossus into the real world. The PCs escape; Asrabey normally takes Kasvarina into the Dreaming. (pp. 401-412)

8. **The colossus finale - Save Flint and begin the next mission.** Borne emerges above Cauldron Hill and threatens the city. Han and Lya depart. The PCs regroup with Aodhan, draw Borne toward the water, and command a small fleet while protecting the king through his banishment ritual. Aodhan sends Borne into the Dreaming, knights the PCs, and tasks them with learning why it was built and how to control or destroy it. Book 6 follows the lead to its gearbuilder, Tinker Oddcog, in Ber. (pp. 413-418)

## Keep the three investigations straight

- **Kell:** dismantle the guild -> locate/capture Kell -> uncover access to the Bleak Gate. He may escape and become a final enemy inside the factory.
- **Ekossigan:** investigate the Cloudwood curse -> stop the child sacrifice -> learn of Kasvarina and gain Asrabey's support.
- **Zubov:** investigate the bombings -> intercept the monster shipment -> prevent the subrail bombing of the summit hotel.

These are concurrent threads, not three consecutive chapters. The banquet is a fallback convergence point; it can be skipped if the party reaches the factory early. Grappa's repair and Dale's briefing support the raid alongside those investigations.
