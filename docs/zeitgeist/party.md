# Zeitgeist party

Build snapshot read from the current Foundry PC sheets on 2026-11-04; all four characters are level 11. These summaries describe the playstyles supported by their current builds, rather than observed player habits. The sheets include local Modern 4e mechanics; consult the live actors for exact rules and later changes.

## Mork — Mork'allande

Mork is an orc Shaman (Stalker Spirit, Voice for the Ravaged) who supports the party by positioning a spirit companion near the fighting, healing allies, and increasing their damage against bloodied enemies. Watcher's Strike improves nearby allies' accuracy, Ironbreaker Claws grants an ally a melee basic attack, and Call to the Blood Dancer creates a strong window for extra damage and critical hits. Infectious Spark and Hateful Binding turn enemies' attacks against other creatures, while the Technologist's Mechanical Pet and Second Self let Mork project powers and senses through a contraption. The build rewards careful companion placement and coordinated focus fire, especially when enabling Gork's heavy melee hits and Idrani's frontline pressure.

Foundry source: `Actor.FlbSPEt2Cfs9J53a`.

## Gork — Gorktholomew

Gork is an orc Barbarian (Rageblood Vigor, Nocturnal) built around fast, hard-hitting charges with a two-handed hammer. Howling Strike supplies repeatable charge damage, Crushing Charge rewards slamming enemies into blocking terrain, and Nightstrider allows charge movement through obstacles while the target remains visible. Swift Charge, Charging Rampage, and Nocturnal Frenzy create opportunities to chain attacks and charges, while Rageblood Vigor and Rising Fury reward bloodying or finishing enemies with temporary hit points and increased damage. Gork thrives with clear targets and offensive support, but the equipped Hammer of the Lost Riders also damages nearby creatures and carries severe critical-hit drawbacks, making spacing and timing important for the whole party.

Foundry source: `Actor.b5jVkNO7OhY5eLEH` (the main-party Gorktholomew, rather than the separate Gork Raging actor).

## Idrani — Idrani Fullhorn

Idrani is a tiefling Fighter (Urban Empath) who protects allies by marking enemies and making movement or attacks against other party members costly. Threatening Rush spreads marks, Footwork Lure adjusts enemy positioning, and Combat Challenge and Combat Superiority punish enemies who ignore Idrani or try to move away. The Guarding Talenta Sharrash, Tail Trip, and Forceful Opportunist reinforce this control through stronger opportunity attacks, knockdowns, and pushes, while Stop Thrust and Master's Edge help intercept threats approaching allies. Outside straightforward melee, Urban Bond supplies investigation through conversations with the city itself, and The City Comes Alive adds an area attack with powerful urban sliding or restraint.

Foundry source: `Actor.vCnL8vlRFYBctiDi`; Fighter identity is recorded in embedded class features even though the actor's class text field is blank.

## Sedridor — Sedridor Wardyn

Sedridor is a human Wizard (Arcanist, Bonded Summoner) who controls battlefield space with summoned creatures, conjurations, walls, and zones. Howling Wall and Beguiling Strands reposition enemies, Web restricts movement, and fire effects such as Wall of Fire and Flaming Sphere make particular routes or positions dangerous. The summon selection offers different tools, including a magma beast for burning pressure, a succubus for domination, and an iron cohort for protection, with Tome of Binding and Careful Summoner improving summoned creatures. The build rewards planning positions and command actions carefully, using Summoner's Slip to reposition after conjuration or summoning powers and coordinating forced movement with the party's frontline and hazardous areas.

Foundry source: `Actor.spP9zXyufTfBLeZZ`.
