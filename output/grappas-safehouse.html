Grappa's Safehouse

The safehouse has been breached. The kidnappers are wheeling Grappa's deactivated golem body toward the back door, while their allies defend the trolley pusher and delay pursuit.

Move Grappa

The Extraction Bruiser pushes the trolley through the connecting doors and across the storeroom toward the rear exit. It barks for its allies to keep the rescuers off it and keeps hauling whenever an opening appears.

If intercepted, it uses Slaver's Tangle against the nearest obstacle and calls for a lookout to take over. Fighting costs it that round's hauling opportunity, so it breaks away as soon as it can resume moving Grappa.

Delay the Direct Rescue

The Gang Blade and western lookout hold off rescuers entering the records room. The Blade marks the most immediate pursuer while the lookout seeks a flank or charges an exposed foe. A successful charge sets up the Blade's Duelist's Poise, allowing it to immobilize the target. The Blade uses Advantageous Jab against marked enemies who attack its allies.

The western Pistol Hunter supports them from behind the low furniture, firing at visible targets and saving Trapping Net for anyone slipping through toward Grappa. It carries the seized writings in a satchel and keeps them close as it retreats.

Contest the Storeroom Shortcut

The eastern lookout challenges anyone trying to reach the carriage through the storeroom. The eastern Pistol Hunter supports it from around the crates, preserving a firing lane while keeping the trolley's route clear.

They concentrate on rescuers racing ahead to bar the rear exit. The hunter uses its net to hold an interceptor while the lookout closes in. If the bruiser is disabled, the nearest lookout takes over the trolley; if the rear door is barred, an available lookout tries to break it open.

Fall Back with the Trolley

As Grappa advances, the defenders yield ground. The western group withdraws through the records room into the storeroom, where the Blade becomes the trolley's rear guard. They abandon opponents who stay behind and close internal doors once their retreating allies have passed through.

Once Grappa is loaded, the gunmen cover the final withdrawal to the carriage.

Morale and Hauling

Priorities: Extract Grappa → delay interceptors → preserve the seized writings → escape.

Only one Haul action can occur per round, whoever performs it.

If Grappa is freed and two standard enemies are down, the survivors flee with whatever writings they already carry. They surrender if cornered.
