# The Mole's Flight

A short ZEITGEIST Book 5 arc before the peace summit, connecting the prepared Grappa safehouse fight and Cloudwood druid fight.

## Campaign situation

Three quarters of the original party died in the eschatologist bombing. The B-Team subsequently died at Kell's hands after the remaining task-force mole compromised Cippiano's deal. A newly assigned A-Team inherits the investigation. The three main investigative threads are already finished; these encounters lead into the summit and the expedition afterward.

The mole believed Kell would use his information to avoid raids and escape Flint. He did not realize Kell would personally slaughter the B-Team. Before learning about those deaths, he sold one final piece of information: Grappa's safehouse address and the arrangements for his protection.

He is guilty, frightened, and trying to escape with his money. His conscience gets him to send a warning; it does not get him to surrender.

## 1. An unsigned warning

During the new team's briefing, Delft receives a folded note:

> Move Grappa. His guards have been reassigned. The orders are false. They are coming today.
>
> I told Kell where the constables would be. I didn't know he would go himself.

The note names the safehouse and the time its protection detail is due to leave. Delft dispatches the party immediately while he checks the reassignment order.

The mole sent this warning before fleeing. It provides an urgent, reliable opening without requiring the new characters to know the previous teams' contacts.

## 2. Rescue at the safehouse

The party arrives as Kell's thugs attempt to remove Grappa. They have false transfer papers and equipment for restraining and transporting his bronze body. Grappa has refused to cooperate, and they are resorting to force.

Run the prepared safehouse encounter.

After the rescue, Grappa identifies the officer who inspected his safehouse earlier that day: the mole. The man asked whether Grappa could recognize everyone who had visited him. Grappa also noticed muddy travelling boots and a green cord around the officer's wrist.

> He said he was leaving on an assignment. He seemed extremely anxious that I should believe him.

Delft confirms that the same officer submitted the false reassignment order and has disappeared. The anonymous warning's reference to the B-Team now makes sense.

Let the party arrange Grappa's immediate safety before pursuing the mole. Delft can personally take responsibility for fresh protection so the players do not have to choose between the two encounters.

## 3. The trail out of Flint

Delft checks the eastern checkpoints while the party secures Grappa. A gate officer remembers the missing man:

> Said he was meeting a witness outside the city. He left with a woman carrying a thornwood staff. Both wore green cords. They took the charcoal road.

The woman is a known Cloudwood guide. The cords identify travellers under her escort. The gate officer supplies her description and the departure time.

On the charcoal road, the party meets a burner bringing his cart toward Flint. He passed the group recently:

> City fellow kept asking when they'd leave. The woman wanted the rest of her money first. They're by the old boundary stones, arguing.

Give these essential directions freely. Tracking, Nature, or local knowledge can earn a shortcut, a concealed approach, or advance warning of the escorts' positions. A failed check should not lose the second encounter.

## 4. What really happened

The party can reconstruct this sequence through the mole's confession and possessions:

1. He gave Kell's people Grappa's address and collected his final payment.
2. As one last errand, he delivered a sealed packet to an Obscurati courier at an ordinary rented room, away from Kell's properties.
3. The courier mentioned the B-Team's slaughter, assuming the mole already knew. The mole realized he was a disposable witness working for people willing to murder constables personally.
4. When the courier turned away, the mole struck him with a bottle, stole his ring, and fled. He had seen the courier use it to whisk an associate out of the way and thought it might help him escape.
5. He sent the warning about Grappa and hired the druids to escort him out of Flint.

The mole fears the RHC, Kell, and the courier's associates. He cannot safely return to anyone he knows.

## 5. The Cloudwood confrontation

The druids have stopped at the old boundary stones to demand the outstanding payment. Their leader increased the price after discovering their passenger is a wanted constable. This dispute gives the party time to catch up.

The leader knowingly escorts fugitives for money. The other druids have been told the mole is fleeing corrupt authorities. Some believe that account; others simply accept the pay.

When the party arrives, the mole tries to keep them talking while edging toward the woodland path:

> I sent the warning. Grappa's alive, isn't he? You got there?
>
> I gave Kell information. He was supposed to clear out before the raid. I didn't know what he'd do.

Mentioning the B-Team makes him defensive:

> If I'd known, I wouldn't have told him. What else do you want me to say?

If confronted about Grappa:

> Yes. I gave them that address too. Before I heard.

The druid leader refuses to surrender a paying passenger. Run the prepared Cloudwood fight, with the mole concentrating on escape. Evidence of his betrayal can shake the less committed escorts even if their leader fights on.

During the fight, the mole uses the stolen ring to help an escort slip out of a bad position, begging them to clear his escape route. This demonstrates the reward before the party acquires it.

## Reward: Ring of the Lingering Guide

*An original homebrew ring intended for the party's shaman.*

A narrow iron ring holds a bead of black witchoil beneath cloudy glass. A tiny handprint sometimes clouds the glass from within. When the ring works, a spectral hand appears beside its recipient and gently draws them to safety. The wearer hears a faint, unfinished word.

**Encounter power — Minor Action**

Choose one ally other than yourself within 5 squares whom you can see. That ally can shift up to 2 squares as a free action.

**Shaman property**

When you use this ring's power while your spirit companion is present, you can determine its range and line of sight from your spirit companion's space instead of your own.

The witchoil's trapped spiritual residue briefly takes shape through the shaman's companion. The power gives the shaman a simple extra support tool: extract an ally from a flank, help a ranged character disengage, or move an ally into position for the companion's benefits. It grants no extra attacks or healing.

The mole can use the ordinary power through an escort he considers an ally. The spirit-companion property becomes useful when the party's shaman claims it.

## 6. Returning before the summit

If captured, the mole explains how he compromised Cippiano's operation and identifies the other information he sold. His purse contains his payment; his belongings include Grappa's address, a copy of the forged guard reassignment, and the courier rendezvous instructions. These preserve the evidence if he dies or refuses to speak.

He knows little about the Obscurati themselves. He can describe the courier and the handoff, but he cannot explain the conspiracy's plans. The ring is a tangible reward and a trace of their methods, without opening a new mandatory investigation before the summit.

Delft can secure Grappa under fresh arrangements, cancel the compromised orders, and give Cippiano an explanation for the failed deal.

The new team reaches the summit having saved Grappa, stopped the remaining mole, and recovered a useful magic item. After the banquet, Aodhan's ritual can take them into the Bleak Gate, with Grappa alive to guide them through the facility.
