CAULDRON HILL COMPLEX V10 - FOUR DUNGEONDRAFT LEVELS

0  Ground - Lower Facility
   Checkpoints, barracks/cafeteria, prototype guardpost, Borne staging
   platform, lower freight lift landings, utility rooms and service runs.
1  Lake Entrance (-300 ft)
   Water tunnel, berth, quays, guard room, hydraulic plant and cargo lift.
2  Upper Borne Chamber (+100 ft)
   Bridge, both upper freight landings, control cabins, crane galleries,
   restraint winches and the catwalk leading to the VIP checkpoint.
3  VIP Suites (+100 ft)
   Checkpoint, dining room, kitchen/locker, laboratories, twelve lodgings,
   upper utility plant and adjacent service passages.

The original v9 coordinates and 174 x 96 five-foot canvas are retained.
Switch levels in Dungeondraft; the sections are no longer all on Ground.
The upper chamber and VIP suite levels are separate as requested despite
sharing the same elevation. Their transfer doorway is retained on both.
Cargo and freight elevator landings retain the earlier atlas coordinates.
No golem artwork is included. Solid floors, cafeteria spacing, replacement
machinery and the lantern layer (300, beneath walls) are preserved.

Native map: cauldron-hill-complex-v10.dungeondraft_map

room-key.json identifies every room's level and original coordinates.

Source: ZEITGEIST Book 5 Part 3, with the existing map adaptations.
Uses the installed Crosshead packs and Baileywiki circular shadows.
Door artwork and wall-shadow paths remain manual. The supplied PNGs are
the repository renderer's approximation; opening in Dungeondraft remains
the final native rendering check.

Rebuild / verify / render (repository root):

node --test output/maps/cauldron-hill-complex-v10/verify.test.mjs
node output/maps/cauldron-hill-complex-v10/render.mjs
