CAULDRON HILL COMPLEX V7

All areas 1-26; no golem depiction. Prototype hallway, vestibules and
control alcoves (Area 5) use solid stone slab flooring instead of grates.

service equipment and anchored restraints, leaving the colossus itself
for a separate token or model. No body-mounted collars or gearing remain.

sump -150 ft; VIP +100 ft; lake dock -300 ft. Insets form an atlas.
