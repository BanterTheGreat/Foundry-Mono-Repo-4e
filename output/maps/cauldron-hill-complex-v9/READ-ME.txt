CAULDRON HILL COMPLEX V9

All areas 1-26; no golem depiction. Prototype hallway, vestibules and
control alcoves (Area 5) use solid stone slab flooring instead of grates.
Borne's chamber retains platforms, lifts, bridge, crane, reservoirs,
service equipment and anchored restraints, leaving the colossus itself
for a separate token or model. No body-mounted collars or gearing remain.

174 x 96 five-foot squares; PNG 11136 x 6144 (64 pixels per square).
Source: ZEITGEIST Act One Book 5 Part 3, printed pp. 405-410.
Two views of the chamber show staging at 0 ft and bridge at +100 ft;
sump -150 ft; VIP +100 ft; lake dock -300 ft. Insets form an atlas.
Equipment dressing and some geometry are adaptations of the book.
