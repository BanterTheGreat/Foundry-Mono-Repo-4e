Three approaches, different costs

  • Centre: intercept the hauler. The tall parts cabinet at (8–9,10) breaks the straight southern approach. Go around either end; the Gang Blade at (8,8) can contest the inner approaches but cannot seal both. Benches at (6–7,9) and (10–11,8) create staggered workstations rather than a continuous barricade.
  • West: pressure the gunman. The bench at (4–5,10) offers approach cover. The gunman at (5,6) fires over the bench at (4–5,7); reach him by going around an end or crossing it. The lookout at (5,8) screens this position but has only 1 HP. The lathe at (3,7–8) breaks sight along the longer outer approach.
  • East: cut off the carriage. Benches at (12–13,10) shelter the early approach. The tall cabinet at (14,7–8) creates a blind corner: pass inside at column 13 or outside at column 15. The eastern lookout contests the turn, while the gunman at (13,4) covers the final approach to the loading door.

Movement check: terrain-only sample paths from the southern starts cost 6 movement to a central square adjacent to Grappa, 7 to the western gunman’s flank, and 11 along the eastern bypass to the square inside the rear door. These count diagonals and difficult terrain, conservatively avoid cutting machinery corners, and ignore enemy occupation. Opportunity attacks, nets, and movement powers change the result. With speed 6, the central route can reach Grappa in one move and leave a standard action to attempt a rescue. It passes adjacent to the Gang Blade and risks an opportunity attack when moving away. The western flank and eastern bypass require additional movement; a speed-6 rescuer who moves twice cannot also spend a standard action to free Grappa or bar the door that turn.

Range and role check: both gunmen have firing opportunities within pistol range 10; the cabinets offer sight breaks along the approaches. Range-3 nets matter when rescuers close rather than covering the entrance. The Blade’s jab needs melee reach, and its mark does not stop movement by itself. Both lookouts have open squares beside their screen positions for flanking or charging as the fight develops.

Objective preserved: Grappa stays at (9,6), with the bruiser at (8,6). The route to (16,3) remains ten squares: five uninterrupted Haul actions. Bar the door with an adjacent standard action, or release the harness using the existing rescue rules. No new damage hazards or extra enemies; the enemy XP rating remains Normal (+1), while terrain and objective pressure are separate.

Bench interaction, clarified: a standard action tips one adjacent one-square bench section; that square becomes impassable and blocks sight until righted with a standard action. It does not flip an entire multi-square workstation. The hauling clock, conditions, and environmental changes remain manually adjudicated.

Workshop density: most benches are two-square work surfaces with aisles between them. The northern dark-blue runs are tall parts cupboards. Tools, hanging hoses, and suspended lamps can fill the visual space without creating more floor obstacles. Flat floor; no elevation bonuses.
