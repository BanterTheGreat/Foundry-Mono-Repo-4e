CAULDRON HILL COMPLEX V2 - ALL AREAS 1-26

174 x 96 five-foot squares. Player PNG: 11136 x 6144 at 64 px/square.
Source: ZEITGEIST Act One, Book 5 Part 3, printed pp. 405-410.
The original version is retained separately.

Area 6 connects directly to Area 5: staging platform at 0 ft, witchoil
sump 150 ft below. Two freight elevators (Area 7) connect this view to
the separate Area 8 bridge view at +100 ft. Both views show the same
upright 300-ft colossus at different cross-sections. Borne is represented
schematically by metal-pattern plates, head apertures and gear joints.

The upper view includes a retractable bridge, central split, two control
cabins, safety rails, barrel positions, restraint chains and service crane.
Its catwalk connects to the relocated VIP checkpoint. The drop from the
bridge to the sump is 250 ft. The speckled black sump represents witchoil
with trapped spirits. The lake dock inset is -300 ft, reached by Area 3's
cargo lift. Insets form an atlas of the complex at different elevations.

Barracks subdivisions, dock footprint, furnishings and some chamber
geometry are adapted from the schematic overview and prose.

Uses installed Crosshead packs and Baileywiki circular shadows.
All doorways use invisible Interior:empty portals; add door art manually.

The PNG is the repository renderer's approximation, not an in-app export.
