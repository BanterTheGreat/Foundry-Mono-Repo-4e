CAULDRON HILL COMPLEX — ZEITGEIST BOOK 5, PART 3

Files
  cauldron-hill-complex.dungeondraft_map — editable native map
  cauldron-hill-complex.png — 7168 x 6144, 64 pixels per five-foot square
  gm-reference.png — numbered overview with elevation / transfer notes
  room-key.json — complete room inventory, coordinates, secret openings

Scope
  Source: ZEITGEIST Act One, Adventure Five, printed pp. 405–410;
  complex overview on printed p. 408 (PDF page 417).
  Areas 6, 7 and 8 (Borne's chamber, freight elevators and construction
  bridge) are omitted. Area 5, the prototype golem guardpost, remains.
  All twelve lodgings (15–26), both labs, five utility plants and the
  one-square-wide service bypasses are retained.

Scale and levels
  Native canvas: 112 x 96 five-foot squares, 560 x 480 feet.
  Lower complex at bottom; VIP wing at top right is +100 ft.
  Parity Lake dock at top left is -300 ft. Its lift matches area 3's lift.
  The sections are an atlas on a single canvas, not adjacent rooms.
  Cross between them by the matching lift/shaft or the omitted chamber.
  The three approach tunnels continue off map; their half-mile lengths
  and exterior gatehouses are not reproduced.

Adaptation
  The book's overview uses ten-foot squares and is schematic. This map
  retains its room ordering and circulation at five-foot tactical scale;
  barracks subdivisions, furniture, dock footprint and some corridor
  dimensions are interpreted for play rather than a pixel-for-pixel trace.
  The former heavy-component hall remains mostly empty, as described.
  No creatures or tokens are baked into the art.

Open in Dungeondraft
  Uses your installed Crosshead packs plus Baileywiki circular shadows.
  Object and wall shadows are off. No generated wall-shadow paths.
  Every doorway uses the invisible Interior:empty portal; add visible
  doors / frames manually. Secret doors are listed in room-key.json.
  Check floor-pattern scale and portal cuts on opening. The supplied PNG
  is the repository renderer's approximation, not an in-app export.
  The application has not been launched or controlled for validation.

Rebuild / verify (repository root)
  node output/maps/cauldron-hill-complex/build.mjs
  node --test output/maps/cauldron-hill-complex/verify.test.mjs

Automated checks pass for inventory, all rooms reachable from their own
section entrance, exact floor coverage, asset manifests, native format,
wall alignment/order, prop scale, clutter calibration and shadow rules.
